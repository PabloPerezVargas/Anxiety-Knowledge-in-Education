# Roadmap

The historical evaluation of immediate assessed learning is complete through
Phase 4E. Its sequence moved from overall pre/post change to content-level
patterns, historical item behaviour, cohort comparability, and exploratory
POST dimensionality. These results generate hypotheses for Track B, but do not
test new items, distractors, or forms.

## Completed through Phase 4E

- Prepared and described the restricted historical dataset (N = 1,090).
- Summarised paired PRE/POST change separately by workshop, with uncertainty
  and cohort descriptions.
- Mapped item-level change and its distribution across participants.
- Examined item-rest relationships and internal consistency separately at
  PRE and POST.
- Checked Anxiety score distributions and centre composition across years;
  comparability of the Anxiety instrument and scoring across cohorts remains
  uncertain.
- Explored POST dimensional structure with Spearman correlations, parallel
  analysis, and limited exploratory factor solutions. Findings are not formal
  validation of a unidimensional scale.

## Next: interpret and consolidate Track A evidence

- Review the historical scoring and workshop-version questions with the
  workshop developer, especially for Anxiety across cohorts.
- Use the [conceptual framework](docs/conceptual_framework.md) and
  [evidence-to-design synthesis](docs/evidence_to_instrument_design.md) to
  carry the historical findings into Track B questions.
- Decide which aggregate analyses and outputs belong in the final compendium.
- Keep all pre/post findings framed as immediate observed change, not an
  isolated causal workshop effect.

## Ongoing: Track B instrument development

Anxiety remains the first priority, followed by Decision-making and later
Risk. New items and A/B forms do not yet exist; no fixed test length has been
decided. Blueprinting, item development, content review, and piloting remain
future work. Historical findings may guide hypotheses, but only new data can
show how new multiple-choice items, distractors, or forms perform.

Counterbalancing two paper forms within a class is feasible in principle, but
empirical form equivalence has not been demonstrated. Formal equating, anchors,
IRT, DIF, and item-bank work are conditional later options, not current
requirements.

## Later and optional work

Independent rescoring, digitisation of any available historical item-level
perceived judgements, and delayed follow-up require further access, resources,
or data collection. Any public release requires a privacy review. See
[methodology](docs/methodology.md) and
[limitations](docs/limitations_and_future_work.md) for design and
interpretation details.
