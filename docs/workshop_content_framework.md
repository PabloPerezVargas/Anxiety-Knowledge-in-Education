# Workshop content framework

This document summarises the broad educational content of the three historical
workshops. It uses public conceptual descriptions only; it does not reproduce
question wording, scoring keys, or private workshop materials. The domains
below help organise discussion of content coverage and are not validated
subscales.

## Anxiety

The Anxiety workshop introduces anxiety and activation, the general process
linking situations, emotional and physiological responses, thoughts, impulses,
and behaviour. It also addresses how learning and automatic responses develop,
metacognitive reflection, verbalisation and support, regulation, prevention,
and using knowledge in practical reasoning.

## Decision-making

The Decision-making workshop addresses decision-making as a process influenced
by deliberative and emotional cognition. Its broad content includes planning,
subgoals, perceived control or competence, impulsive responding, organisation
of information, considering alternatives and support, and integrating
information before acting.

## Risk

The Risk workshop covers activation and distress, psychological precursors of
risk, impulsivity, metacognition, regulation during crisis, prevention and
protection, support seeking, and reflective rather than immediate responding.

## Measurement boundary

The three workshops address different content and use separate historical
instruments. An item number in one workshop does not necessarily assess the
same concept as the same number in another, and raw item or total scores are
not a common psychological scale. These descriptions are content frameworks,
not published test specifications or scoring guidance. The future blueprint
should be built from confirmed workshop objectives and reviewed materials.
